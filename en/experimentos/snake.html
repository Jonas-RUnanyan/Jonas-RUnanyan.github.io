What it is

The starting point was a 2D procedural animation experiment: a movement controller in which each segment of the snake's body follows the head smoothly and continuously, rather than moving cell by cell on a rigid grid. From that controller, the mechanics of the classic Snake game were built: body growth when eating, self-collision detection, and resetting the game on collision.

Music generation

On top of that base I added a procedural music system, with the idea of crossing two areas of interest: music and algorithmic content generation. The system generates chords in real time, choosing each new chord based on its consonance with the previous one using probability rules and the circle of fifths. The soundtrack isn't prerecorded: it's generated during the game and changes with every playthrough.

Technical notes

  • Animation resolved through segment-to-segment follow calculations, with no sprites or premade keyframes.
  • Music generation based on music theory rules (circle of fifths, chord consonance), not simple randomness.
  • Solo development, from start to finish, in Processing.
  • Pending: a basic user interface for the game.
